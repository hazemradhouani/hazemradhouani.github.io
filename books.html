Academic | Thesis | 2024

Between the Poetics of Place and the Value of Heritage

To Revive the Spirit of Al Erg Village, Hammat Al Jarid

بين شاعرية المكان و قيمة الأثر

Institution
ENAU | Université de Carthage
Supervisors
Prof. Moncef Fourati | Prof. Adnen Ben Nejma
Distinction
Mention Très Bien à l'unanimité | June 2024

In Between the Poetics of Place and the Value of Heritage, I trace the silent dialogue between clay walls and desert light in Al-Arg village, where every curve of adobe and joint of stone carries the memory of generations. Taken on a journey through space and time, this mémoire d'architecture unearths the sensory bond villagers share with their landscape, reveals how humble materials embody sustainability, and weaves in my own childhood recollections among Al-Arg's lost courtyards. More than a study of form, it is a tribute to a living heritage, an invitation to rediscover a place where earth and sky converse, and a blueprint for nurturing the delicate harmony that sustains both community and craft.

Degree thesis in architecture submitted at the National School of Architecture and Urban Planning of Tunis, Université de Carthage. Awarded Highest Honours with a unanimous jury. The project proposes a rehabilitation strategy for the abandoned ksar village of Al-Arg in the Jérid oasis system of southern Tunisia, working simultaneously across territorial, urban, and architectural scales.

The research draws on heritage documentation carried out with the Société de Préservation du Village d'ARG over two years prior to the thesis, grounding the design work in direct material knowledge of the site. The theoretical framework engages the Italian school of urban morphology and the practices of Carlo Scarpa and Giorgio Grassi.
