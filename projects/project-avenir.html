Studio BELEM / Building permit, December 2025 / Housing, shop and car park

Project

The flood-risk prevention plan (PPRI) requires the whole building to be raised to 29.5 NGF, about 1.6 m above natural ground. The permit notice turns this into a plinth of bonded, perforated brick on which the two blocks sit: two offset low walls holding a continuous planter, so that the street is partly planted at passers-by's height. On the plinth, the active uses of the raised ground floor are arranged within a covered passage, wide open to the street, that links the two blocks; a semi-buried car park lies beneath, about 2.0 m below natural ground. A shop, at natural ground level, occupies the south end of block A, at the corner of avenue de Verdun and rue de l'Avenir.

The materials notice names bio-sourced materials (timber windows, French windows and curtain walls; timber decking or slabs on pedestals for the terraces), a geo-sourced substrate for the planted roofs, and reused solid brick.
